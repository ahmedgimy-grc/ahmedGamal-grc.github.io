01 · Method

How I assess a control

For the NCA ECC self-assessment I rated every control, named an owner, and listed the evidence that proves it. The console on the right replays that loop.

  1. 01

    Scope

    Pick the framework and the systems in scope, then map each requirement to its sub-controls.

  2. 02

    Rate

    Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant or N/A, judged on how the control runs today.

  3. 03

    Evidence

    List the exact proof an auditor will ask for: configs, logs, tickets, signed policies.

  4. 04

    Remediate

    Every gap gets an owner and an action, agreed with the control owner.

  5. 05

    Track

    Follow each finding to closure and report it with KPIs and KRIs.
